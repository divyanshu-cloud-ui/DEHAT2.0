Turning the site into one continuous story, told in illustration.

Twenty-two new illustrations arrived. They are not decoration for the pages we have — they are a cast, a set of symbols, and a line that can run through the whole site. This is the plan for using them, section by section, with the motion each section needs and the reason a visitor will stop rather than scroll.

02

The yellow path is the site’s spine

Two of the new files are not pictures of anything. They are hand-drawn ochre paths, and they are the most useful things in the set. Used once, a path is an ornament. Used as one continuous line that starts in the hero, runs behind every section, and ends at the invest form, it becomes the thing a visitor is following.

The bar at the top of this page is the mechanic, drawn with the actual asset: it fills as you read and it is visibly unfinished. That is the whole trick. An incomplete line is an open loop, and open loops are uncomfortable to abandon.

The path also solves the navigation complaint: because it is continuous, arriving mid-page from a button feels like a jump along a line you already know, not a teleport into a wall of text.
