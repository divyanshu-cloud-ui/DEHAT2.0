Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 22 September 2026

DEHAT collects only information needed to operate this website, process a contribution, issue receipts, meet statutory obligations and answer a request. We do not sell personal information or use advertising cookies.

Information we collect

  • Donation and receipt details such as name, email, phone, postal address, PAN when an 80G receipt is requested, contribution purpose and transaction reference.
  • Information voluntarily included in partnership, volunteering, complaint or contact messages.
  • Essential device storage for language, theme and cookie preferences. Optional performance information is used only when consent is given.

Why and how it is used

Information is used for payment reconciliation, donor communication, Form 10BD/10BE and accounting obligations, fraud prevention, audit, responding to requests and maintaining the security of DEHAT's services. Payment-card and bank credentials are processed by Razorpay and are not received by DEHAT.

Sharing and retention

Access is limited to authorised DEHAT finance, audit and administration personnel and service providers needed to perform these functions. Information may be disclosed where required by law. Records are retained for the applicable accounting, tax, audit and regulatory period and securely disposed of afterward.

Your choices

You may ask what information DEHAT holds about you, request a correction, withdraw optional cookie consent, or raise a privacy concern. A lawful retention requirement may prevent immediate deletion of statutory financial records.

Security and children

DEHAT uses role-based access and reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Child-protection case information is not collected through the donation form and is handled through restricted safeguarding processes.
